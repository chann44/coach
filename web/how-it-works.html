How Coach works

Coach connects to your daily signals, builds context, and nudges you over iMessage with clear next actions.

Step 01

Connect sources

Link calendar, health, mail, and selected tools you already use.

Step 02

Read context

Coach checks events, tasks, sleep, workouts, and relationship signals.

Step 03

Prioritize your day

It identifies what matters now: blockers, energy constraints, and key follow-ups.

Step 04

Text in iMessage

You get concise, actionable messages where you already respond fastest.

Step 05

You reply naturally

Short replies like "do it", "remind me", or voice notes update the plan.

Step 06

Close loop nightly

Coach tracks outcomes and prepares your next day with better timing.

No dashboard overload. Just relevant nudges and execution.
