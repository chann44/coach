Privacy First

Your life data stays yours.

Coach is designed to run locally on your Mac whenever possible. We only request what we need to help you, and you can disconnect any integration at any time.

What we collect

  • Account basics (email and waitlist metadata).
  • Integration tokens you explicitly connect.
  • Messages and events required to power reminders, planning, and coaching.

How we use data

  • Generate proactive coaching messages in iMessage.
  • Help with planning, follow-ups, health context, and task execution.
  • Improve reliability and prevent abuse.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not run ad targeting using your private messages.
  • We do not access integrations you have not explicitly connected.

Controls

  • Disconnect integrations anytime.
  • Delete history and request account deletion.
  • Disable specific categories (health, work, relationships, finance).
